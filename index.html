The Philosophy

Yoga as movement science — not a trend

Bloomlife Yoga is built on evidence-based, integrated yoga: a disciplined blend of movement science, breath and cognitive focus designed to support how the body and mind actually work. Every session is intentional, measured, and grounded in decades of study and practice.

Evidence-Based

Practices chosen for what the science supports — mobility, strength, balance, nervous-system regulation and sustained cognitive function, rather than fads.

Integrated & Whole

Movement, breath and mind are trained together, so yoga becomes a woven part of everyday living — not an isolated hour on the mat.

Personalised

Sessions are tailored to each woman's body, history and goals — including adapted, supportive practice for those with altered abilities.

About Shweta

Shweta Bala

Certified Yoga Instructor · Jaipur, Rajasthan, India

Shweta is a certified yoga instructor from Jaipur, Rajasthan, who has practised yoga for over 30 years. Her teaching is rooted in a deep, lifelong discipline — one that treats yoga as a precise, restorative science rather than a passing routine.

She is also a certified support therapist, integrating movement science and cognitive exercise to support people with altered abilities — helping each person move, think and live more freely.

Shweta graduated with a science degree and pursued yoga for more than five years in India, going on to study other complementary health-science disciplines before making Australia her home. That blend of formal science and traditional practice shapes every Bloomlife session.
